Worship & Music

Christ-centered, Scripture-saturated singing that leads our church family into God's presence every Sunday.

Overview

Leading the church in heartfelt worship

Our worship team leads the congregation in Christ-centered, Scripture-saturated singing every Sunday morning. Whether you play an instrument, love to sing, or serve behind the scenes on sound and tech, there's a place for you on the team.

We believe worship is for everyone in the room, not a performance — our goal every week is to help the whole church family respond to God together.

  • • Vocalists, musicians, and tech/production volunteers welcome
  • • Weekly rehearsal ahead of Sunday morning worship
  • • Auditions not required — just a heart to serve

Good to Know

A few quick answers

Do I need to audition?

No formal audition — reach out and we'll set up a time to meet and rehearse together.

How often is rehearsal?

The team rehearses weekly ahead of Sunday morning worship.

What if I don't sing or play an instrument?

We also need help on sound, media, and stage — no musical background required.

When & Where

Sunday Mornings, 10:00 AM

The worship team leads both weekly services, with rehearsal earlier in the week. Reach out to learn the current rehearsal schedule.
