Bible Study

Sunday morning is just the beginning — real community happens throughout the week, in homes across Rancho Cordova.

Overview

Life together, beyond Sunday morning

Our small groups meet throughout the week — in homes and at the church — to dig into Scripture, pray for one another, and build the kind of friendships that carry you through every season of life.

New groups form each season, so there's always a good time to jump in, whether you're brand new to FBC or have been part of the family for years.

  • • Groups meeting weekly, in homes across Rancho Cordova
  • • Wednesday night Bible Study & Prayer at 6:30 PM in the Fellowship Hall
  • • New groups forming each season — everyone is welcome

Good to Know

A few quick answers

When do groups meet?

Most groups meet weekly, on a range of weeknights — Wednesday Bible Study & Prayer is at 6:30 PM.

Do I need to know the Bible well?

Not at all — groups welcome everyone from brand-new believers to longtime students of Scripture.

Can I host a group?

Yes — we're always glad to help new leaders open their home and start a group.

When & Where

Weekly, Throughout the Week

Wednesday Bible Study & Prayer meets at 6:30 PM in the Fellowship Hall. Additional small groups meet in homes on various weeknights.
