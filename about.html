About Our Church

A family of faith,
not just a building.

Who We Are

Bible-teaching. Family-centered.

First Baptist Church of Rancho Cordova is a Bible-teaching church centered on prayer, fellowship, evangelism, and discipleship. We are committed to building genuine relationships and caring for one another as a faith family.

Whether you're a new believer, growing in your faith, or looking for a place to serve, you'll find a welcoming community here — one that takes Scripture seriously and takes care of one another even more seriously.

What We're About

Four commitments that shape everything we do

Prayer

We believe prayer is the lifeline of the church — personal, corporate, and constant.

Fellowship

We do life together, caring for one another as a genuine, extended family.

Evangelism

We share the good news of Jesus Christ boldly, locally and around the world.

Discipleship

We grow together in Christlikeness through Scripture, mentorship, and practice.

What We Believe

The Baptist Faith and Message

Our church has adopted the Baptist Faith and Message of the Southern Baptist Convention (2000) as our statement of faith. It reflects what Baptists have historically believed, rooted in Scripture alone.

I. The Scriptures

The Bible is God's revealed truth, without error, and is the supreme authority for faith and life.

II. God

The eternal, triune God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — perfect in holiness and worthy of all worship.

III. Man

Every person is made in God's image, possessing dignity, yet fallen and in need of redemption.

IV. Salvation

Salvation is offered freely by grace, through faith in Jesus Christ alone.

V. The Church

A local body of baptized believers, joined in covenant to worship, serve, and carry out the Great Commission.

VI. Baptism & the Lord's Supper

Two ordinances observed in obedience to Christ, pointing to His death, burial, and resurrection.

VII. Evangelism & Missions

Every believer is called to share the Gospel, locally and to the ends of the earth.

VIII. The Family

Marriage and family are gifts from God, designed to reflect His love and order.

IX. Religious Liberty

Every person has God-given freedom to worship — or not — according to conscience.
