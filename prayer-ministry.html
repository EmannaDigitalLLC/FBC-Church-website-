Prayer Ministry

Our prayer team intercedes for our church, our city, and our world — and would be honored to pray with you too.

Overview

The lifeline of our church

We believe prayer is the lifeline of the church — personal, corporate, and constant. Our prayer team meets regularly to intercede for our church family, our city of Rancho Cordova, and needs around the world.

If you have a need, we would be honored to pray with you — before or after any service, or any time in between. No request is too big or too small.

  • • A dedicated team praying for church, city, and world
  • • Prayer available before and after every service
  • • Confidential prayer requests welcomed anytime

Good to Know

A few quick answers

How do I submit a request?

Reach out through our contact page, or speak with any greeter or pastor before or after a service.

Are requests kept confidential?

Yes — our prayer team treats every request with care and discretion.

Can someone pray with me right now?

Prayer team members are available before and after every Sunday service.

When & Where

Every Sunday, Before & After Service

Our prayer team is available before and after both Sunday gatherings. Requests can also be submitted anytime through our contact page.
