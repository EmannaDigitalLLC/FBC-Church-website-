Student Ministry

Real conversations, real friendships, and a real look at following Jesus through the middle and high school years.

Overview

A place to belong, ask questions, and grow

Middle and high schoolers gather weekly for worship, teaching, and small groups led by adult leaders who genuinely care about their lives — school, friendships, faith, and everything in between.

We don't shy away from hard questions. Our goal is to help students build a faith that's truly their own, one that can stand up to real life.

  • • Weekly student gatherings with worship and teaching
  • • Small groups led by caring, trained adult leaders
  • • Seasonal retreats, camps, and service projects

When do students meet?
